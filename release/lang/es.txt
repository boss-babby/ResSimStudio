Add fractures: 'Draw' in the plan view (click-drag a trace), '+ Add fracture', or type coordinates in the\n   table. Drag the end points of the selected trace; drag its top / bottom in the section view.\n3. Each fracture is cut by the matrix columns and layers into fracture cells (EDFM, Moinfar et al. 2013):\n   f-m transmissibility 2 A k_n / <d>, f-f along the trace and at intersections, cubic-law w^2/12.\n4. Wells whose completions lie in a cell cut by a fracture are connected to it automatically\n   (WI_f = 2 pi k_f w / ln(r_e / r_w)). 'Check fractures' lists the connections before you run.\n5. Optional mechanics: Barton-Bandis closure under the effective normal stress from the stress field,\n   thermo-elastic unloading on cooling, Coulomb hydro-shear dilation (irreversible).\n6. Run (F5).
